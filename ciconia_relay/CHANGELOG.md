# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- The tracker ports are now **7171** (TCP) and **7172** (TLS), instead of
  5027 and 5028. Point your trackers at the new port and forward it in your
  router.

## 0.2.0

- Each tracker links to its device page in Home Assistant.
- The panel is about Home Assistant only.

## 0.1.0

First version: trackers over TCP and TLS, the panel in the sidebar, Home
Assistant through MQTT (Mosquitto broker app) or the Ciconia integration.
